## Description

Aging is inevitable. Being misled about it isn’t.In this episode of Plastic Surgery Uncensored, Dr. Rady Rahban breaks down the true biology of aging — and why it’s far more complex than just “wrinkles.” Collagen and elastin decline. Fat shifts and disappears. Bone resorbs. Muscle thins. Circulation changes. Then you layer in sun exposure, stress, lifestyle, and genetics. Aging isn’t one event — it’s a cascade happening simultaneously.Dr. Rahban explains what can realistically slow the process —
