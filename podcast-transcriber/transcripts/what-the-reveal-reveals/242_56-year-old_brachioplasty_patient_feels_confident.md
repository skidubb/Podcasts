## Transcript

Our wonderful patient who's 56 years old is about four months out from his arm brachioplasty. He had had significant weight loss which left him with significant excess skin on his arms. He comes in today in follow-up getting ready for an upcoming trip which will be exciting but he wants to share some of his thoughts and experiences about the entire process and thank you very much for doing so. Yeah my pleasure. I mean quite simply having lost a lot of weight you end up with this surplus skin and you don't get to enjoy the fact that you've lost all the weight. From my perspective I was almost paranoid about my underarms. It stopped me wearing t-shirts and and the like and it was like I hadn't finished the journey so I eventually got it done. Not really knowing what to expect but at the end of the day all I was going to be happy with was being able to wear a t-shirt again. Ultimately I've ended up in a position where I'll be happy taking my shirt off entirely and going to the beach so certainly very happy with the results. Thank you so very much for sharing your story. Pleasure.
