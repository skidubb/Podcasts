## Description

This patient was born with two different sized breasts that became more asymmetrical with age. She would compensate for this by wearing very tight bras all the time. Dr. Claytor fixed this issue by putting different sized implants in each breast and performing a lift on the larger breast. The patient was worried that her breasts would look too large after, but she loves the size and feels that they look very natural. Thank you for tuning in to Plastic Surgery: What the reveal reveals!
